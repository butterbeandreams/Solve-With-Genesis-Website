12-Week Progressive Program

Stationary Cycling & Strength Training

Three phases, twelve weeks, zero guesswork. A structured plan that balances progressive strength training with zone-based cycling workouts — designed to take you from base fitness to peak performance.

!

Safety Note: This is a general fitness program, not individualized medical advice. Consult a physician or qualified coach before starting if you are injured, pregnant, managing a medical condition, or new to structured training.

The Side Quest

A personal project on training, recovery, and systems that hold up over time.

Different domain. Same belief: lasting performance comes from a system people can actually follow — progressive phases, repeatable sessions, measurement, recovery, and consistency rather than random intensity.

Phase 1

Foundation

Weeks 1–4 · Low intensity · Habit-building

Build your aerobic base and establish movement quality. Lighter loads, higher reps, and Zone 2 cycling to create a sustainable training habit. Week 4 is a deload.

Phase 2

Build

Weeks 5–8 · Progressive overload

Increase volume and intensity. Introduce more structured strength progressions and add controlled intensity on the bike while protecting recovery.

Phase 3

Peak

Weeks 9–12 · Performance focus

Sharpen strength and cycling capacity. Higher quality sessions, clearer intensity targets, and deliberate recovery so the work sticks.

How to use this

Simple rules that keep the system honest

  • Train most days, recover on purpose. Strength 3×/week, cycling 3–4×/week, one true rest or active recovery day.
  • Zone 2 is the base. Easy enough to talk in full sentences. That is where the endurance bank is built.
  • Progress the plan, not your ego. Add load or volume only when form is solid. Missed sessions are skipped, not “made up” in one day.
  • Deload is part of the program. Planned easier weeks keep you training for months instead of burning out in weeks.
